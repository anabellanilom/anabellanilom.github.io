Research

My research agenda focuses on political representation and gender, cabinets and ministerial recruitment, political parties and political finance, with an emphasis on Latin America. I work with comparative politics approaches and the production of evidence for academic debates and public policy discussions.

Master’s thesis

Presidential strategies, participation, and integration. Women’s appointments to Latin American cabinets (1999–2019) (UTDT, 2021).
Comparative analysis of 18 countries (1999–2019) on gender integration in presidential cabinets.

    Universidad Torcuato Di Tella (UTDT) | 2021–2022
